# Human gates

At a gate the implementer halts, writes `GATE-<id>.md` in the repo root with the evidence bundle below, commits it, pushes, and waits. Allowed responses: `proceed`, `proceed-with-rescope: <text>`, `stop`. The implementer (an AI agent) never buys anything, never connects to the bike and never merges to `main` itself.
